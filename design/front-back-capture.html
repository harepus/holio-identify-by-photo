Holio side challenge, pain 3

Front and back in one identify call

Copy follows PR 4 (minimal copy): titles and essential controls only. The side labels Front and Back, and the "Back photo" button, carry the instruction.

Serves Holio pain 3: "Items without a barcode, or with one the system does not know. Recognise the product from a photo or packaging text." The capture screen asks for the front and the back before the first identify call, so the size and variant print reaches the model in the same call.

Why

The arena ran the frozen recogniser p3-final on the blind set fresh3: 69 cases, 49 of them in the 249-row register.

Photos up front match the follow-up accuracy within run-to-run spread, save the second round trip and nearly halve the median wait. Today the product sends one photo unless the worker thinks to add a second, which is the 27/49 row. Sources: holio-arena/pains/p3-no-barcode/experiments/capture-fresh3/RESULT.md and holio-arena-holdout/p3-no-barcode/scores/fresh3/p3-final-single.json.

The product caps a call at 2 photos and keeps that cap. The arena gave 3 photos in 22 of 69 cases, so 2 photos is not measured directly.

Unchanged

  • The contract: photos takes 1 or 2 images, the answer is match, choose or none with at most 3 candidates, and every HTTP error keeps its code.
  • No hidden second call, no automatic retry and no follow-up capture. The worker decides when to send.
  • A photo suggestion never writes a receipt or links a barcode. Only the explicit Confirm or Review receipt action commits.
  • "Add a photo of the size" on the choose screen still replaces photo 2, and its label there reads "Size" instead of "Back".
  • Dark and blurred photo errors, the network error, cancel and manual search.

Not built: the pre-selected first suggestion

The second proposal was to pre-select the first suggestion on the choose screen. On fresh3 the recogniser was unsure with suggestions in 18 cases. The first suggestion was right in 6 of them. In the other 12 the item was not in the register at all, or the label disagreed. The confidence score does not separate the two groups: the three most confident unsure answers (0.9) were all wrong. Pre-selecting would put a wrong product one tap from a receipt in 2 of every 3 unsure answers, so the choose screen keeps asking the worker to pick.
