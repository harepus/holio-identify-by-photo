Oslo Sprint 2026 · Design proposal · 7 November

Receiving that keeps moving

Serves Holio’s brief: “show a solution that makes the day easier for the people who work in the warehouse”, pain 3: items without a usable barcode. Design jury: user-tested, more than one happy path, universal design.

English throughout, matching the public Holio Ops screenshots. Holio’s Android orange actions, white inventory cards and receiving data model guide this proposal. Mobile receiving and scan feedback are not public; these interactions are our design, not a claim of an existing Holio screen. No old “Holio Ops 3” header.

Review artifact, not working app code. All 390 × 844 phone states below are inspectable. Product drawings and the non-grocery order are synthetic. Buttons illustrate the proposed actions. This has not yet been tested by a warehouse worker.

What was wrong

  1. Manual search is a dead end: it only says “Holio opens its product search here.” A worker who cannot get a photo match cannot finish. Screenshot before-03-search-placeholder.
  2. Counts cannot be corrected or reviewed: no Undo, quantity entry or location choice. Known codes can pass their expected quantity without an explicit warning. Confirm counts before the separate Link barcode prompt. Screenshots before-08-match, before-09-link, before-11-next-scan.
  3. The good-scan banner vanishes after 2.5 s, has no product image and omits “+1”. With Demo tools expanded, simulated scan buttons scroll the camera result above the visible area; the next-scan screenshot shows a green frame but no visible count. After linking, the instruction and count compete. Screenshot before-10-return.
  4. Small links and an icon-only Scan action are hard to discover with a box in one hand. Back/reload targets and recovery links fall below 48 px. Screenshot before-02-scanner; recovery links measured 22.5 px high, Back 40 px, Reload 34 px.
  5. Eight order lines need scrolling; the scanner’s 56% viewport plus header, navigation and recovery links pushes demo controls below the fold. Primary Scan remains visible, but photo actions and fallback content compete for space. Screenshots before-01-order, before-05-capture.
  6. “Confirm”, “Link barcode” and “Register deviation” do not make the receiving consequence obvious. Extra receipt is hidden behind a barcode-link label. Desktop tools and inactive Picking navigation distract from the task.
  7. Obsolete green-dot header and black primary actions differ from current brand direction. Order starts Under received even with zero received. English flow retains Norwegian catalog/warehouse names; those are data, not a translated UI. No English/Norwegian UI mix observed in the default English path.

Tap audit, from open scanner: known scan 0 app taps; unknown photo with one image 5 app taps (Identify by photo, capture picker, Identify, Confirm, Link barcode), plus OS camera actions. Two photos add another picker tap and OS actions. Missing barcode omits linking: 4 app taps plus OS actions. Demo simulation adds two setup taps, excluded. Proposed unknown-photo path: Identify by photo, Take photo, Use photo, Review receipt, Receive = 5 taps; improvement is usable recovery, quantity/shelf safety and removal of a post-count decision, not an invented tap reduction. Batch receive avoids one scan per unit.

Screen and state inventory

One orange primary where a commit or clear next step exists. Equal recovery choices intentionally have equal neutral treatment. All touch targets at least 48 px, primary actions at least 56 px; fixed footer includes bottom safe-area space. Long lists may scroll, but key actions never require scrolling at 390 × 844. At large text sizes, allow content scrolling rather than clipping.

Interaction contract

Pause reads while any sheet, photo, review or save is active. Known in-order scans increment one unit only when location is known and the line is not full. Require the code to leave view before rearming it, including after photo recovery. Use a receipt operation ID to prevent retries from doubling counts. Ignore late identification results after cancel. Keep the latest successful receipt and Undo reachable; older corrections live on the order line.

Use ink text on orange and pale status surfaces; status meaning also appears in text. Use 16 px body text, 17 px product names, 14 px secondary SKU text. Announce receipt updates without stealing focus. Trap focus in sheets, restore it to the initiating control, and label icons. Honor reduced motion. Sound/haptics are optional, never required. The 390 × 844 layouts reserve safe areas; verify actual iPhone keyboard and Zebra glove behavior before claiming universal usability.

Demo data, without application changes

Swap demo/purchase-order.json to synthetic grooming, cable, car accessory, outdoor and packaging lines using its existing fields: lineId, sku, name, brand, size, expected, received, image and barcode. Use notOnOrder for a 5 m cable beside an ordered 3 m cable. Mirror products into demo/register/register.json and provide matching local product images. This is a data-assets change, not app/server code. It is not honestly a single JSON edit if the register and images still contain groceries. Restart only the isolated demo server to reindex the new catalog.

Keep supplied code values clearly synthetic; a missing code stays absent, never presented as a real GTIN. Existing demo code generates fallback EANs for missing barcodes, so demonstrate the missing-code path via No barcode, not a fabricated printed label. Locations require the planned receiving schema change; adding them to JSON alone does not make today’s app show them. Keep grocery evaluation results separate from the new category demo. Holio has not published test data.

Decisions and user test before implementation

Proposed default: never receive an item outside the PO; set it aside. Confirm with Holio whether its deviation workflow permits an extra line, and who may approve over-receipt. Confirm how a barcode maps unit versus case quantity. Obtain permission for real product photos or use team-owned objects. The demo order can be synthetic; no customer data is needed.

Ask Sebastian to receive a known item, an unknown code, a missing code, a wrong-size cable and a batch of six with one hand, then undo a mistake. Record completion, errors, time and whether he can state what was received and where. Repeat in gloves and with larger text. This is the required next user test, not claimed completed evidence.

Sources and audit limits

Local evidence: Holio Ops research, 7 November, its App Store/Google Play images and desktop help-receive-po-02.webp; project README, app README, existing two designs and shell sources. Observed desktop receiving establishes quantity, location, split allocation and additional barcode semantics; it does not establish mobile interaction details.

Audit: production build on isolated 127.0.0.1:8774 after “hpi warm”, Chromium at 390 × 844, fake-camera barcode and uploaded fixture photos. Live identification returned HTTP 503 after 1.9 s; the success/link path and ambiguous, absent, dark, error and timeout states were inspected using labeled mock modes. Fake-camera known barcode ran without mocked identification. Canned match text is independent of the uploaded fixture: these mock screenshots test layout and transitions, not recognition accuracy. Browser automation is not physical iPhone or glove validation. Design is awaiting user/strategy go before implementation.
